Digital Plant Identification App

This client-facing progressive web app was built for The Sculpture Park at Waitakaruru Arboretum to replace an outdated printed plant catalogue. Visitors can identify plants through image-assisted workflows, browse curated records, and access content designed for on-site use.

The app combines React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS with AI-powered plant identification integrations and searchable plant data. A major requirement was strong usability in low-connectivity environments.

My Contributions

I implemented offline-first functionality using IndexedDB so users could still browse and search plant information without internet access. I also integrated external AI services, connected CSV-backed datasets, and contributed to a C# admin panel that enabled botanists and staff to manage records and trigger deployment workflows.

The project sharpened my client communication, product delivery, and full-stack implementation skills, especially around practical UX for real-world usage conditions.
