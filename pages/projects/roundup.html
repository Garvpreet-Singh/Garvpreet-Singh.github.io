RoundUp

RoundUp is a personal finance web application that automatically rounds up everyday purchases to the nearest dollar and invests the accumulated spare change into a user's Sharesies investment account.

The platform combines Open Banking transaction data with roundup calculations, a persistent ledger, and transfer generation to make investing effortless and transparent. It is designed around real user control, strong financial visibility, and practical automation.

How It Works

  • The user securely connects their New Zealand bank account through Akahu.
  • RoundUp retrieves recent transactions and calculates eligible roundup amounts.
  • Spare change is accumulated daily and prepared for transfer to Sharesies.
  • Sharesies auto-invest can then invest deposited funds based on the user's investment plan.

Tech Stack

Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite, TanStack Start, Tailwind CSS

Backend (planned): Node.js/Express, PostgreSQL, Render

APIs: Akahu Open Banking, Sharesies bank transfer funding, Akahu Payments (planned)
