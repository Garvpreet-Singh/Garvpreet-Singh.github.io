About Me

I was born and raised in Hamilton, Waikato, and I'm currently in my final year of a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Software Engineering at the University of Waikato.

I'm passionate about building software that solves real problems and creates meaningful experiences for the people who use it. Whether I'm developing full-stack web applications, cloud-integrated systems, or experimenting with new technologies, I enjoy the challenge of turning ideas into practical, reliable solutions. I'm particularly interested in learning how different technologies work together and continuously expanding my skills as new tools and frameworks emerge.

One of the aspects of software engineering I enjoy most is working with others. I like collaborating with clients and teammates to understand their ideas, gather requirements, and transform them into software that delivers real value. Throughout my studies and industry experience, I've learned that successful software isn't just about writing good code, it's about communication, problem solving, and understanding the people you're building for.

When I'm not programming, I enjoy working on personal projects, spending time with friends, and exploring places around New Zealand. Whether it's a drive to Raglan, relaxing at the beach, or simply playing video games at home, I believe maintaining a healthy balance outside of software helps keep me curious, motivated, and ready for the next challenge.
